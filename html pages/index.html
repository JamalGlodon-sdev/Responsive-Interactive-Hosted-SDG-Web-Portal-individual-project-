Life on Land — SDG 15

Sustainable Development Goal 15 focuses on protecting, restoring, and sustainably managing terrestrial ecosystems. It aims to combat deforestation, desertification, land degradation, and biodiversity loss while protecting wildlife and natural habitats.

Why Biodiversity Matters

Healthy Ecosystems

Healthy ecosystems provide resources and services that people, animals and plants depend on.

Protecting Life on Land

Protecting biodiversity helps preserve habitats, species and natural resources.
