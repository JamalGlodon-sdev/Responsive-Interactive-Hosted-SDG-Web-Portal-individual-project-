What is SDG 15?

SDG 15, Life on Land, calls for the protection, restoration and sustainable management of land-based ecosystems. It is one of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals that 193 countries adopted in New York on 25 September 2015. Target 15.3 aims for a land degradation neutral world by 2030.

Key Facts

Forests

Forests cover about 30 per cent of the Earth's surface. Besides providing food security and shelter, they are crucial for fighting climate change, protecting biodiversity and supporting Indigenous communities.

Forest Loss

Forest area fell from 31.9 per cent of total land area in 2000 to 31.2 per cent in 2020. The UN also reports that about 13 million hectares of forest are lost every year.

Desertification

Ongoing degradation of drylands has led to the desertification of 3.6 billion hectares. Human activity and climate change both drive deforestation and desertification.
